## Pitfalls hit tonight

- `runlogs/runpod/smoke/pods.txt` empty after the shutdown → "all 0 pool pods" forever.
- A `cmd1 && cmd2 && nohup x &` line runs the whole `&&` chain in a background subshell: variables exported in it never reach the foreground shell (two launches went out with an empty `--tro`).

# Duplicate models in the task object selection

Status: checked 2026-09-11 for every model listed in `task_object_selection.csv` / `.xlsx` (793 models, 161 categories; the custom Kohs block `toy_dice/isgblk` is not a BEHAVIOR asset and was skipped).

## Why

BEHAVIOR-1K contains assets that share one mesh under several model ids (relabelled bottles, recoloured cups, the same object stored in two orientations). The grasp-library selection only avoided visually identical *pairs* of test picks (pair rule); nothing checked the candidate pool. Counts of "viable models" therefore overstated geometric diversity.

## Method

1. **Geometry signature** (`geometry_signatures.json`): the visual meshes of every model are read from the decrypted USD (`usd-core`), transformed to the object frame, and summarised rotation-invariantly: vertex/face count, surface area, volume, sorted bbox extents, area-weighted PCA eigenvalues, a D2 pairwise-distance histogram of 1500 surface samples (fixed seed) and a hash of the sorted vertex distances to the centroid.
2. **Pair tiers** within a category (rel = |a-b|/max): `identical` = same vertex hash (same vertex set up to a rigid motion); `same_shape` = extents <= 2 %, volume <= 1 %, area <= 2 %, D2 L1 <= 0.06 (same shape, different tessellation or tiny edits); `near` = extents <= 3 %, volume <= 4 %, area <= 4 %, D2 <= 0.10 (shape family). Clusters = connected components at tier `near` or better (`geometry_clusters.json`). Diffuse textures are compared by md5 and a 24x24 thumbnail distance as hints.
3. **Visual review**: every model of every cluster was loaded in OmniGibson and rendered from three fixed views at the same camera distance relative to its bbox; one grid per cluster (`duplicates/clusters/<cluster>.png`, one row per model). Seven reviewer agents judged each grid: `exact_same` (same geometry and same surface appearance; orientation / tessellation differences allowed), `texture_variants` (same geometry, different colour / label / material) or `different_geometry` (false positive of the geometric check), and listed the `exact_groups` of visually indistinguishable models (`verdicts.json`, raw reviewer files in `duplicates/review/`).

## How the sheet uses it

* Models in an **exact group are treated as one model**: the header-row and category-block counts in `task_object_selection.csv` / `.xlsx` count an exact group once, and every member row says `exact duplicate of ...` in the `duplicates` column (red cell in the xlsx).
* **Texture / colour variants are kept** as separate models (that is fine for training) but every member row says `same geometry as ... (texture variant)` (blue cell in the xlsx) so the geometric redundancy is visible.
* Counts are written as `M (N)`: M = distinct geometries (every flagged cluster - exact duplicates, texture / colour variants and the engraved alphabet-block family - counted once), N = distinct models after folding only the exact duplicates. Category blocks are ordered by M, descending.
* Clusters judged `different_geometry` (only the alphabet blocks) get no per-row annotation beyond their exact sub-groups, but count as one geometry in M.
* **Disembedding distractor pools** (`scripts/build_disembedding_pools.py`, `select/disembedding_distractor_pools.json`, spec section `disembedding-0.distractors`): the exact groups are the seed of the *look-alike groups* - models that cannot be told apart from the robot's initial view (~0.9 m, objects 20-130 px tall) and are therefore never drawn as each other's distractors. The look-alike review is stricter than the duplicate check: it adds same-shape variants that differ only by a small print / label / shade (e.g. the black Dorset cereal boxes sgirte / wdkcbu, the white 172 mm shampoo bottles lvvkhx / stjjjm, the beer bottles that differ only by a label on one side), different-geometry models of the same colour and size class (plain white mugs 70-96 mm, grey teaspoons 123-147 mm, white oval mice) and whole categories whose members are indistinguishable at that scale (the 41 mm alphabet blocks, the black cookie cutters, the yellow tortilla chips). Every group and its reason is listed in the `same_category_pool` rows of `task_object_selection.csv` and flagged `LOOK-ALIKE` in the duplicates column of the disembedding target rows; judged on `duplicates/clusters/*.png` and the V2 / V3 tiles of `strips/*.png`. Not reviewed: label-dependent distinguishability of single-label bottles at arbitrary yaw beyond the groups above.
